Table 1. Baseline characteristics of the study cohort (n = 32,574 ICU stays), stratified by incident delirium. Continuous variables are median (Q1, Q3) and compared with the Mann-Whitney U test; categorical variables are n (%) and compared with the chi-squared test, or Fisher exact test when an expected count was below 5. SMD, standardised mean difference. Because the sample is large, |SMD| > 0.1 is used to flag a meaningful difference rather than the p value alone.
